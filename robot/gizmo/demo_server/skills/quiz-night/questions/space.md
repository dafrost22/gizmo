# Round: Space

Ten questions. For each: the answer, what else to accept, and a line to say afterwards.

1. Which planet is the biggest in our solar system?
   - Answer: Jupiter.
   - After: About 1,300 Earths would fit inside it.

2. Which planet is the hottest?
   - Answer: Venus.
   - Not Mercury, though it's closer to the Sun.
   - After: Its thick clouds trap the heat, at about 465 degrees Celsius, or 870 Fahrenheit.

3. How long does sunlight take to reach Earth?
   - Answer: about 8 minutes.
   - Accept: 8 minutes 20 seconds, 500 seconds, anything from 8 to 8 and a half minutes.
   - After: So we see the Sun as it was 8 minutes ago.

4. Who was the first person to walk on the Moon?
   - Answer: Neil Armstrong.
   - After: On July 20, 1969, with Buzz Aldrin a few minutes behind him.

5. What is the name of our galaxy?
   - Answer: the Milky Way.
   - After: It holds somewhere between 100 and 400 billion stars.

6. How many planets are in our solar system?
   - Answer: eight.
   - After: Pluto has been a dwarf planet since 2006.

7. Which planet spins on its side?
   - Answer: Uranus.
   - After: It's tipped over by about 98 degrees, so it rolls around the Sun.

8. Roughly how long does the International Space Station take to go once around the Earth?
   - Answer: about 90 minutes.
   - Accept: an hour and a half, 92 minutes, anything from 85 to 95 minutes.
   - After: So its crew see about 16 sunrises every day.

9. What is the closest star to Earth?
   - Answer: the Sun.
   - If they say Proxima Centauri: that's the closest after the Sun, 4.2 light-years away. Count it as wrong, with a smile.
   - After: The next nearest, Proxima Centauri, is 4.2 light-years away.

10. Which planet has a storm called the Great Red Spot?
    - Answer: Jupiter.
    - After: That storm is wider than the whole Earth.
