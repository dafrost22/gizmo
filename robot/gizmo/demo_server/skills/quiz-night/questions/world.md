# Round: The world

Ten questions. For each: the answer, what else to accept, and a line to say afterwards.

1. What is the capital of Australia?
   - Answer: Canberra.
   - Not Sydney or Melbourne.
   - After: It was built as the capital because Sydney and Melbourne couldn't agree which of them it should be.

2. Which is the largest country in the world by area?
   - Answer: Russia.
   - After: It stretches across eleven time zones.

3. Which country's flag is a red circle on a white background?
   - Answer: Japan.
   - After: The circle is the Sun. Japan is called the land of the rising Sun.

4. What is the tallest mountain on Earth, measured from sea level?
   - Answer: Mount Everest.
   - After: Its summit is about 8,849 metres up, or 29,032 feet.

5. Which is the largest ocean?
   - Answer: the Pacific.
   - After: It covers about a third of the planet's surface.

6. Which is the smallest country in the world?
   - Answer: Vatican City.
   - Accept: the Vatican, the Holy See.
   - After: It's smaller than half a square kilometre, inside the city of Rome.

7. How many continents are there?
   - Answer: seven.
   - Accept: six, if they explain that they count Europe and Asia, or the Americas, as one. Schools in some countries teach it that way.
   - After: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia or Oceania, Europe, North America and South America.

8. Which is the largest hot desert in the world?
   - Answer: the Sahara.
   - After: The largest desert of any kind is Antarctica, because a desert is a place with almost no rain or snow.

9. What is the capital of Canada?
   - Answer: Ottawa.
   - Not Toronto, Montreal or Vancouver.
   - After: Toronto is the biggest city, but Queen Victoria chose Ottawa as the capital in 1857.

10. Which country has the only national flag that isn't a rectangle or a square?
    - Answer: Nepal.
    - After: It's two stacked triangles, with a moon in one and a sun in the other.
